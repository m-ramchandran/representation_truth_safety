Single-source probes have stable out-of-domain performance from 68% depth
onward, but earlier-layer performance differs by model and training regime.
For a given target dataset, method and depth, we first take the median AUC
over the probes trained on each of the other datasets. For each combination of
model, framing, regime, dataset, depth, we then take the best AUC over the ten
single-source classification methods, and plot the median over datasets to
show the typical dataset's best achievable out-of-domain AUC per training
regime. Median best-method AUC across datasets is shown at 53%, 68%, 88%, and
100% depth for (a) within-target, (b) domain-absent, and (c) one-source
training; color is the model and line style the prompt framing. Within-target
AUC varies by at most 0.02 across depths. For transfer performance (panels b
and c), Qwen at 53% depth trails its best later layer by 0.16–0.21 AUC,
whereas Gemma trails by at most 0.08. From 68% onward, consecutive depths
differ by at most 0.03.
