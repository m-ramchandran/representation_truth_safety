Prompt framing is clearly distinguishable from the leading activation PCs. PCA
is fit on the joint set of 430,230 original and True/False framed activations
at 88% depth per model, colored by (a) dataset, (b) framing, and (c) label.
Original and True/False prompts occupy distinct regions in both models whereas
labels have more overlap. Framing geometrically shifts activations more than
the label does, limiting direct probe transfer across templates.
