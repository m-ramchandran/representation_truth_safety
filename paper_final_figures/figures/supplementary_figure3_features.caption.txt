Alternative activation-based features do not improve on raw activations,
though some come close. (a) Best-run AUC after replacing each activation
vector with a 100-bin histogram, which discards neuron identity; bars run from
chance to the raw-activation AUC and the loss is printed at right. Binned
features are nearly lossless on Geometry of Truth and the easier safety
datasets but lose more significantly on the unique or difficult datasets. (b)
AUC difference relative to raw activations for within-target and one-source
probes using alternative features at 68–100% layer depth. PCA keeping 95% of
the variance is effectively lossless; dictionary learning features (SAE,
transcoder), and PCA + dictionary features lose 0.01–0.05 AUC, binned features
lose about 0.10.
