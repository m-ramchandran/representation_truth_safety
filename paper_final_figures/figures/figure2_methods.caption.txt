Linear probes match nonlinear alternatives across training regimes. Results
are aggregated over all combinations of classification methods, datasets,
source models, framings, and depths; regimes become less target-relevant from
left to right. (a) Best-depth AUC per method; bars show the interquartile
range and the dashed line the model output-logit AUC. (b) Paired AUC
differences between different types of approaches. Random slopes, a linear
probe with dataset adjustments, is counted as a linear method. Lines are
medians over all combinations of factors listed above, and bands show the
IQR. The best nonlinear and linear probes differ by at most 0.011 median AUC
in every regime. Probes beat output logits by 0.08 AUC within target but by
only 0.001 under one-source transfer.
