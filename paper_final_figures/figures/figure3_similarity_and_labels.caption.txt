Probe predictions largely agree across methods, and averaging them adds
little. All panels pool both models and prompt framings. (a) Spearman
correlation (ρ) between two probes' predictions for linear–linear,
linear–nonlinear, and nonlinear–nonlinear pairs in each training regime
(medians and inter-quartile ranges); agreement is relatively high (ρ =
0.77–0.92). (b, left) Averaging the methods beats the median single method
only slightly, and falls short of the best single method. (b, right) Neither
uniform nor oracle-weighted ensembles of other datasets' probes recover
within-target performance (medians over models, framings and depths). Each
probe direction cannot be fully captured by the others.
